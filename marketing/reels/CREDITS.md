## Desk reels (Blender, MacBook on a desk)

`blender-desk-get-in.mp4`, `blender-desk-ask.mp4`, `blender-desk-see.mp4`. Rendered in Blender (Cycles, AgX), screen = real recordings of the PaperManager frontend against a mock that replays public data (arXiv 2206.00364, AlphaFold 3 teardown). Typefaces: Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL); the app logo is PaperManager's own.
