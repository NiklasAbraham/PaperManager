## Text

```
What happens when a paper goes into my library?

This is the AlphaFold 3 paper, taken apart: 24 pages split, 16 figures cut out, ten claims and 48 authors pulled from the text, then put back together as one card.

Then it ties into everything else. Twenty papers in my library that cite it connect to it, eight join my thesis project, and 27 more on structure prediction line up behind its topic.

So the next time I search, chat or write a report, the paper is not a PDF in a folder. It is a node in a graph that knows what it belongs to.

That is PaperManager, open source and self-hosted: https://niklas-abraham.de/projects/papermanager/

Every title, count and link in the film comes from the real library.

#OpenSource #Research #KnowledgeGraph #SelfHosted #AlphaFold
```
