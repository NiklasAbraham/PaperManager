# LinkedIn posts 4-9: the Blender reels (short, video only)

Status: draft, not posted. Niklas posts them himself. Post 4 carries the paced AlphaFold 3 reel; the other five are the
same size, one per reel, a few days apart. Each is video only, no images.

Posting order: 4 paced, 5 catalogue, 6 get in, 7 ask, 8 see, 9 fast (the 20-second one works as a Friday post).

All counts come from the AlphaFold 3 entry of the live library (read-only dump, 12 Nov 2026): 24 pages, 16 figures,
10 claims, 48 authors, 4 topics, 10 concepts, 20 library papers that cite it. 1,173 is the library listing.

## Post 4: the paced reel

Attach: `../reels/blender-af3-paced.mp4` (1080x1920, 40 s, with sound), poster `../reels/blender-af3-paced-poster.webp`.

```
One paper goes in. This is what comes out.

AlphaFold 3, 24 pages: a summary written for me, 16 figures cut out with their page numbers, 10 claims pulled from the text, 48 authors, 10 concepts, and the 20 papers in my library that cite it. Then all of it folds back into one card.

That card is how I read now. It is PaperManager, the open-source paper library I build next to my thesis.

Free and self-hosted: https://niklas-abraham.de/projects/papermanager/

What is the first thing you would want a paper to give you?
```
